How we help

A clear path from issue to next step.

We focus on understanding the issue first, then choosing the most appropriate support route.

STEP 01

Tell us the issue

Describe what happened and share only the screenshots or details needed to understand the problem. Never share your password or login codes.

STEP 02

Review & troubleshoot

We assess the available options and work directly on suitable cases, or explain the steps for simpler issues.

STEP 03

Follow the right process

We guide the next steps using legitimate platform processes. Final decisions remain with the relevant platform.
